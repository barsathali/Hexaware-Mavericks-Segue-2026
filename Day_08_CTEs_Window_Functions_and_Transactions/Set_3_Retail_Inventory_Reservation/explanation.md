# Retail inventory reservation explanation
`PlaceRetailOrder` locks the product row, checks quantity, decrements stock, inserts the order and order line in one transaction. The sample asks for 99 monitors when stock is 5; THROW causes rollback, leaving stock at 5 and no partial order. The correlated subquery compares each product to the average stock in its category. The CTE aggregates product sales and uses ROW_NUMBER with PARTITION BY Category to return the top three products per category. LAG and LEAD show previous and next month's units; current minus previous gives month-on-month change. MongoDB `$inc` is atomic for one document. Use a multi-document transaction when the operation must also create separate order/order-line documents or update several product documents as one all-or-nothing operation.
